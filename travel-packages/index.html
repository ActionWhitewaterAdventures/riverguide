Fly & Drive Choice

Boise to Boise Package

$495 / person
*$195 single supplement fee if traveling alone

When flying or driving into Boise, Idaho, we recommend this stress-free bargain package. We make hotel and travel arrangements to get you from Boise to your river trip and back to Boise at the end.

Pre-River Trip Arrangements
  • Lodging: Double occupancy lodging in Boise, ID at the Vista Inn. Continental breakfast available starting at 5:15 am.
  • Charter Flight: Scenic morning flight from Boise to Salmon, ID. Air services provided by Gem Air.
Post-River Trip Arrangements
  • Lodging: Double occupancy lodging at Third Street Inn in McCall, ID. Continental breakfast available starting at 6:00 am.
  • Airport Return: 8:30 am shuttle transportation from McCall back to Boise, ID (airport).

Note: You will need to arrange your own commercial travel to Boise, Idaho by air or car prior to the start of the package.

Road Trip Choice

Salmon to McCall Package

$375 Car Shuttle + $195 / person Hotel
*$170 single supplement fee if traveling alone

When driving directly to Salmon, ID, we recommend this driving package. It provides 2 nights lodging (one night in Salmon and one night in McCall) plus delivery of your vehicle directly to the river trip’s end in McCall.

Pre-River Trip Arrangements
  • Car Shuttle: We arrange to shuttle your personal car from Salmon to McCall, ID. Cars are left at Stagecoach Inn (keys will be collected before boarding the river bus).
  • Lodging: Double occupancy lodging at Stagecoach Inn in Salmon, ID. Continental breakfast available starting at 6:15 am.
Post-River Trip Arrangements
  • Lodging: Double occupancy lodging at Third Street Inn in McCall, ID, where your car awaits. Continental breakfast available from 6:00 am to 11:00 am.

Note: If you prefer to drive back from Salmon at the end of your trip, call our office for charter flight arrangements from McCall to Salmon. Additional charges apply.
