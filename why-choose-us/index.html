Pioneering River Outfitters

Industry Firsts on Idaho Rivers

For over 45 years, Action Whitewater Adventures has set the standard for environmental responsibility and equipment safety on the Main Salmon River. Here are four practices we introduced to Idaho wilderness rafting.

First Idaho Outfitter

Fire-Safe Propane Cooking

We were the first outfitter in Idaho to transition to clean, fire-safe propane cooking systems on wilderness trips. This practice eliminated open wood fire hazards and protected fragile river canyons from wildland fires.

First Idaho Outfitter

Human Waste Carry-Out Systems

We introduced portable, sealed containment systems on the Main Salmon River to ensure human waste was packed out completely. This innovation preserved pristine sandy beaches and river water quality for future generations.

First Idaho Outfitter

Fire Pans for Beach Protection

We pioneered the mandatory use of elevated metal fire pans to catch charcoal and ash. This practice keeps white sand beaches completely clean and uncharred without leaving a trace of camp presence.

First Idaho Outfitter

Patented Solar UV Water Purification

We engineered and implemented patented solar UV purification systems on our river trips. This equipment provides clean, fresh drinking water directly from river sources without reliance on single-use plastics.
