5-Day Main Salmon River Expedition

5-Day Wilderness Trip Details

Journey 82 miles through Idaho's legendary River of No Return canyon, featuring thrilling Class III and IV whitewater, white sand beach camps, geothermal hot springs, and freshly cooked Dutch oven meals.

Expedition Length
5 Days / 4 Nights
River Miles Covered
82 Miles Total
Whitewater Level
Class III & IV Rapids
Meeting Location
Salmon, Idaho
Choose Your River Experience

Versatile Rafting Options Every Day

Every guest can choose how they want to ride the river each day. You can mix and match between different craft styles throughout your 5-day journey.

Most Popular

Guided Oar Boats

Sit back, relax, and let your expert guide handle the oars. Perfect for soaking in the scenery, taking photos, and navigating big wave trains with maximum comfort.

High Energy

Guided Paddle Boats

Join a team of 6 to 8 paddlers working together under the direct leadership of a professional guide calling commands through major rapids like Black Creek and Bailee.

Independent Thrill

Inflatable Kayaks ("Duckies")

Take full control of your own single or double inflatable kayak in calm pools and medium rapids. A fun way to get close to the water under guide supervision.

Day-by-Day Journey

5-Day Sample Wilderness Itinerary

A preview of a typical 5-day Main Salmon River trip. Campsite locations vary by water levels and group preference.

Day 0

Orientation in Salmon, Idaho

Meet your lead river guides the evening before launch for final preparations.

  • Pre-trip orientation and safety briefing
  • Waterproof dry bag distribution
  • Hotel stay in Salmon, Idaho
Day 1

Launch Day & Upper Canyon Rapids

Shuttle from Salmon to the Corn Creek put-in, load gear, and launch into the canyon.

  • Scenic shuttle ride with a quick store stop
  • Exciting upper canyon whitewater rapids
  • Riverside beach camping and relaxation
Included Meals: Deli Sandwiches Lunch Steak Dinner Strawberry Shortcake Dessert
Day 2

Black Creek Rapid & Hot Springs

Enjoy morning hot coffee, tackle big whitewater, and soak in natural hot springs.

  • Navigate Black Creek and Bailee Rapids
  • Soak in natural geothermal hot springs
  • Camp games and evening relaxation
Included Meals: French Toast and Pork Tuna Salad Lunch Pasta Dinner Peach Cobbler Dessert
Day 3

Historic Ghost Town & Buckskin Bill's

Paddle continuous rapids and take guided shore excursions to historic pioneer homesteads.

  • Hike through an authentic mountain ghost town
  • Visit historic Buckskin Bill's ranch for ice cream and history
  • Sandy beach camp in the canyon
Included Meals: Beignets and Omelettes Riverside Deli Lunch Gourmet Burgers Cheesecake Dessert
Day 4

Floating, Swimming & Talent Show

Relax with calm river floats, canyon swimming, and classic beach camp entertainment.

  • Calm floating stretches and river swimming
  • Beach camp activities
  • Evening wilderness talent show
Included Meals: Pancakes and Bacon Picnic Lunch Chicken Fajitas Dinner Chocolate Cake Dessert
Day 5

Lower Canyon Float & Charter Return

Enjoy a final morning float through lower rapids to Carey Creek take-out before returning home.

  • Scenic lower canyon river float
  • De-rig rafts at Carey Creek take-out
  • Charter transfer back to McCall
Included Meals: Bagels, Eggs, and Sausage Take-Out Deli Lunch
Wilderness Living Standard

Fresh Dining & Camp Comfort

We believe multi-day wilderness rafting should be comfortable, clean, and delicious. Here is what you can expect in camp each day.

Dutch Oven Wilderness Cuisine

Our guides take great pride in preparing fresh, hearty meals using clean propane stoves and campfire Dutch ovens. Every meal includes fresh ingredients, cold beverages, and warm homemade desserts every evening.

Clean Environmental Equipment

We provide heavy-duty 3-person tents, cozy sleeping bags, comfortable cots, solar UV purified drinking water, and clean portable restroom facilities that are set up daily in private spots at camp.

Trip Preparation

What Is Provided & What To Bring

We supply all essential river and camping equipment so you can travel light and hassle-free.

What We Provide

  • All rafting gear, oars, paddle boats, and inflatable kayaks
  • Coast Guard approved life jackets (PFDs) and safety equipment
  • Spacious 3-person tents, cozy sleeping bags, and comfortable cots

Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need previous whitewater experience?

No previous experience is required. Our professional guides handle all boat navigation and provide complete safety briefings before entering any whitewater rapids.

What are the age requirements for kids?

Children as young as 4 to 6 years old can join Main Salmon River trips depending on their independence level. It is one of North America's premier family multi-day rafting trips.

How do car shuttles and travel work?

We coordinate charter flights from Boise to Salmon, Idaho as well as vehicle shuttle transfers to return your car to McCall by the time your trip ends.

How do group discounts work?

Groups of 8 or more adults booking together automatically receive a 10% discount on standard trip rates. Contact our office to secure charter launch dates.
