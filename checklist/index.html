Before You Go

Make sure you have everything you need and know what to expect before launching onto Idaho's legendary Main Salmon River.

Preparation Roadmap

Pre-Departure Milestone Timeline

Follow these four simple steps prior to arrival to guarantee a smooth and stress-free wilderness launch.

30 Days Out

Forms & Dietary Info

Complete your guest registration forms, sign online risk waivers, and submit any dietary requirements or medical notices.

14 Days Out

Travel & Flights

Confirm flight routes into Boise or Salmon, ID, arrange vehicle shuttle services if applicable, and reserve pre-trip hotel lodging.

7 Days Out

Gear Check

Review the packing list, test quick-dry river clothing and broken-in footwear, and prepare essential personal medications.

Day Before

Orientation Meeting

Attend our 7:00 PM pre-trip orientation in Salmon, Idaho to receive your waterproof river dry bags and meet your trip leaders.

Essential Documents & Cash

Government-issued ID or Driver's License
Cash for guide gratuities (typically 10-15% of trip fare)
Completed AWA digital guest health waiver
Flight, hotel, and vehicle shuttle confirmation details

River Apparel & Footwear

2-3 pairs quick-dry synthetic shirts and shorts
Sturdy river sandals with heel strap or water shoes
Wide-brim hat or baseball cap with retaining strap
Lightweight rain jacket & pant layer (breathable)

Personal Essentials & Care

Prescription medications (packed in personal day bag)
Water-resistant sunscreen (SPF 30+) & lip balm
Polarized sunglasses with retainer strap (Chums/Croakies)
Biodegradable camp soap/shampoo & small camp towel

Camp Comfort & Hardware

Compact headlamp or flashlight with extra batteries
Reusable insulated water bottle with carabiner clip
Camp footwear (lightweight sneakers or dry sandals)
Portable battery bank for phone cameras (no outlets on river)
Orientation & Meeting Location

Salmon, Idaho Rendezvous

All Main Salmon River expeditions begin with a mandatory pre-trip orientation meeting the evening before launch.

  • Meeting Time: 7:00 PM (Mountain Time) the evening prior to Day 1.
  • Meeting Place: Stagecoach Inn, Salmon, Idaho.
  • What Happens: You will receive your heavy-duty waterproof dry bags, review river safety procedures, and address any last-minute questions with lead guides.

Good to Know

Wilderness Living Guidelines

No Cell Coverage

Cell phone signals disconnect shortly after leaving Salmon, ID. Emergency satellite communication is maintained by our trip leader staff throughout the canyon.

River Dry Bags

We provide two waterproof dry containers per guest: one large bag for camp gear/sleeping attire and one smaller ammo can for items needed while on the rafts.

Weather Variances

Salmon River weather can range from hot 90°F+ sunny afternoons to cool 50°F canyon mornings. Layering quick-dry synthetic clothing is key.
